## Design Notes and Tradeoffs
- Phase 1 keeps nearby systems as marker/label primitives, not full per-system body catalogs.
- Proper motion is applied as an astrometric linear drift approximation.
- Distance is currently from catalog parallax and treated static in this phase (no RV-derived distance evolution).
- Data source remains external-generated CSV for reproducibility and explicit provenance.
- Frontend gracefully degrades (no backend catalog => no interstellar markers).

## Future Work (Recommended)
