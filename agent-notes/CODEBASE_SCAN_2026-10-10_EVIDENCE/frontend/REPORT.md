## New confirmed findings

### F-NEW-1 — Planet shader applies cube-face transform after sphere projection (P1; all three branches; high confidence)

**Active shipped path:** main.ts:324–329 instantiates `PlanetQuadtree` for Earth and Mars. Moon rendering is disabled. Quadtree geometry is not a dormant demo.

**Mechanism:** `src/frontend/features/planetary/quadtreeLOD.ts:94–109` attaches a shared z=0 plane to face/chunk transforms; root faces have translations ±0.5 at :151–163. `planetShaders.ts:166` normalizes the raw plane vertex, whose z coordinate is always zero, before the face/offset transform. It multiplies that vector by `uRadius` at :173, then applies the complete model-view transform at :201. The ±0.5 face translation is therefore applied as world AU after the tiny radius projection. Root vertices map to circles/disks in the original plane before face placement, not a sphere tessellation.

**Reproduction:** `delivery-results.json.geometry` evaluates the exact matrix ordering for Earth radius 0.001670210938369994 AU, vertex (0.5,0,0), zero elevation, identity planet transform. Result is (0.001670210938369994,0,0.5), distance 0.5000027895967968 AU, **299.365×** the intended radius. This is a CPU reference evaluation, not GPU vertex readback. Terrain displacement is a small radius-scale term and cannot remove the unscaled 0.5 AU face translation. Source inspection additionally establishes that zero-center and pole-cross-product normalizations need finite handling; no GPU NaN claim is made.

**Expected/actual:** face and chunk coordinates must determine the unit-sphere direction, followed by physical radius and planet world transform. Current shader uses raw plane direction and applies chunk placement afterward.

**Closure:** independently verify face centers/corners, shared seams, all subdivision levels, world translation/rotation, finite center/pole normals, and rendered/transform-feedback radius bounds. Fixing LOD merge alone cannot repair this coordinate error.

### F-NEW-2 — Quadtree merge drops all previously split face meshes (P1; all three branches; observed/high confidence)

**Mechanism:** `quadtreeLOD.ts:53–59` removes `this.mesh` from its group without clearing it. `merge` destroys children (:86–91), then `createMesh` exits because that detached mesh still exists (:94–95). No mesh is reattached.

**Reproduction:** both entries in `browser-results.json.quadtree`: construct radius-1 Earth tree with camera (0,0,100), move to (0,0,2) and update, return to (0,0,100) and update. Attached meshes: **6 → 36 → 0**. All six root nodes end with `meshPresent: true`, `meshParentPresent: false`, `split: false`. This is actual Three.js scene-graph execution in the browser. Shipped Earth/Mars run the same `update` at main.ts:1422–1424; the probe isolates camera-distance lifecycle from unrelated shader defects.

**Expected/actual:** zooming back out should restore six coarse faces. Current merge leaves the entire tree without visible geometry in this case.

**Closure:** near/far/near/far cycles preserve full surface coverage, correct parent ownership, bounded mesh/material counts, and no gaps across multiple LOD boundaries.

### F-NEW-3 — Realistic scale excludes the active Earth/Mars quadtrees (P1; all three branches; observed/high confidence)

**Mechanism:** `main.ts:699–712` has an empty quadtree branch in `applyScaleMode`; `PlanetQuadtree.baseRadius`, `uRadius`, displacement and group scale remain at their initial display values.

**Reproduction:** `scene-results.json.scale`, both distinct trees, executes the actual checkbox change handler. Earth's shader/base radius remains **0.001670210938369994 AU** instead of **0.00004258750455597227 AU** (39.22×). Mars remains **0.00004073420277632334 AU** instead of **0.0000226574080509289 AU** (1.798×). Jupiter and Sun mesh scales correctly change to physical values, providing controls for the handler behavior.

**Expected/actual:** the same physical-scale policy must apply to every body representation. The checked control currently changes conventional meshes and belts but leaves terrestrial quadtree radius uniforms unchanged. This is independent of F-NEW-1; correcting vertex projection still leaves this mode inconsistent.

**Closure:** toggle both directions and verify effective surface radius, displacement, LOD distance thresholds and camera framing for Earth/Mars and conventional meshes. Hidden Moon remains a future integration case.

### F-NEW-4 — Earth/Mars are omitted by double-click body picking (P2; all three branches; observed/high confidence)

**Mechanism:** main.ts:1296–1307 raycasts `bodyMeshes` with `recursive=false`. Earth/Mars entries are `Group`s (:326–329), which do not raycast their child meshes. Additionally, `bodyId` is stored on the group (:352), while child patches have no body ID. Merely enabling recursion would not finish the identity/displaced-surface fix.

**Reproduction:** `scene-results.json.picking`: actual objects/camera/raycaster after focusing each target yield Earth=0 hits, Mars=0 hits, Jupiter=1 hit for the shipped nonrecursive call. Earth/Mars child IDs are all null. This isolates hit testing; it does not depend on screenshot interpretation.

**Expected/actual:** the canvas promises double-click-follow for a body; Earth/Mars cannot be selected by this handler. Explorer selection and Follow remain available.

**Closure:** end-to-end double-click follows each displayed planet across coarse/fine LOD and both scale modes, without selecting unrelated displaced patch geometry; resolve ancestor identity or provide deliberate body pick proxies.

### F-NEW-5 — Default-scale solar flare/CME expires on first update (P2; all three branches; observed/high confidence)

**Mechanism:** `solarFlares.ts:225–228` computes lifetime as `(0.3 - sunRadiusAu) / max(0.0001, velocityAuPerSec)`. The initial display Sun radius is **0.3755114512235279 AU**, making lifetime negative. `update` marks it expired at :265–266 and the active flare manager removes it at :329–335. sunController passes current display radius into both random and AGN-response flares.

**Reproduction:** `delivery-results.json.flareLifetimes` constructs actual events with identical default settings and updates 1/60 s: display-scale event changes from not expired to expired; physical-radius 0.00465474539672041 AU event remains alive. Sources are identical between branches. This is lifecycle reproduction, not a visual-duration measurement.

**Expected/actual:** display scaling must not erase an event before its first useful frame. Current default presentation makes the entire flare/CME event immediately eligible for removal.

**Closure:** positive lifetime and bounded expansion in every supported presentation scale, including actual AGN and physics-driven spawning; distinguish physical speed/time from visualization radius explicitly.

## Coverage and limits

Source review covered the actual main bootstrap, DOM/style, scene setup/camera/control wiring, model-worker send/receive/fallback and Float64/availability contracts, time controller, texture helpers, quadtree/shader integration, solar controller/physics/realtime/observation contracts, magnetic/flare/feature/layer resource paths, asteroid/KBO rendering lifecycle, and trajectory transport/planner presentation. Numerical orbit/asteroid/SMBH semantics and backend/PFSS computation are assigned to other auditors.

The wider `src/frontend` sweep inventories all files and checks their bootstrap reachability and lifecycle/network/event boundaries. Additional inspected library/dormant surfaces: alternate animationLoop/inputHandler/uiBindings/cameraController; labels/orbit/route/LOD rendering; local and nearby system loading/scene cleanup; cloud and moon texture pipelines; satellite worker/snapshot orchestration; Milky Way rendering; trajectory controller and API hydration. These are not all exercised by the shipped entry UI. Specifically, main does **not** import the alternate general animation loop, planner controller, SimulationClient, local/nearby systems, cloud pipeline, satellite system, moon texture pipeline, or Milky Way renderer. Their issues must not be reported as directly accessible current controls. Dormant numerical helpers and every shader formula were not independently verified line-by-line.

Preserved behavior: both distinct trees bootstrapped with 10 major-body options, verified 100,000-row asteroid source status, no pageerrors with available WebGL, and actual pause keeping the displayed date constant in the sampled interval. Main client recovery and source/epoch/schema guards are meaningful improvements; no claim is made that these replace end-to-end observed-data or scientific validation.

The report supplies independent roots and actionable closure tests. It does not claim a complete pixel audit, complete line coverage, absence of other defects, or readiness for release.
